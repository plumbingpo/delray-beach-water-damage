Where Water Damage Starts in Miami Properties

Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. We have ordered these five common Miami calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Shut the house supply, run a tap to drain the lines and phone us immediately, whatever the hour. Ceilings that bulge with trapped water should be left alone and the room below kept empty.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Close the small valve under the sink or behind the appliance and the flow usually stops. The harm continues, though, since water has already run under cabinets and flooring where air does not move. For Miami kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Moisture under a raised floor is seldom an emergency, but it should not be put off for a season. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Left alone, the damp air rises into the rooms above and the wood framing begins to decay. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Since growth can start 24 to 48 hours after materials get wet, an old leak that was never fully dried is a likely culprit. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Miami.

Found Water in Your Miami Home? Start Here

The first hour is when good intentions cause the most trouble. Here are the slip-ups we run into most on Miami jobs, with the better option for each.

Do not use a household vacuum on water. You risk a shock and a dead vacuum, and a mop with old towels does the job safely. Leave ceiling fixtures off if the ceiling above is wet, and keep away from any part that droops. Do not lift tacked-down carpet yourself, since it can shrink or tear and may be saveable if it is handled properly. Do not turn the heat up high to dry things out, because warm, damp air is what mold likes.

Do shut off the water, open cabinet doors and closets so air can reach them, and hang up wet fabrics and cushions to drip. Do take pictures before moving anything. Do keep children and pets away from the area. Most of all, phone early, so a Delray Beach Restoration crew is already heading for Miami while you work through this list.
